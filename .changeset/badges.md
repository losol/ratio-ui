New `@eventuras/ratio-ui/badges` (beta): Open Badges on the page. `AchievementBadge` draws an Open Badges achievement as a medallion — four shapes, three metals, a glyph or a symbol in the middle, a caption — with its name, criteria and the date it was earned; without a credential it is locked, or `in-progress` with the way there around the rim; `tile`, `row` and `art` layouts. `AchievementBadgeArt` is the medallion alone as self-contained SVG, for the page or for the file to bake. `Diploma` is a sheet in a double frame — eyebrow, title, recipient, text, the achievements as badges, signatures and the small print — that prints as it shows. New `--badge-*` and `--diploma-*` tokens; Tabula thickens the frames. The data and baking from `@eventuras/ratio-ui-core/badges` are re-exported.
