New `@eventuras/ratio-ui-native/charts` (beta): the web's `Chart` for the app, from the same model in `@eventuras/ratio-ui-core/charts` and the same palette — `Chart.Legend` (`interactive` to fade a series out and back), `Chart.Plot` with `Chart.Grid`, `Chart.Bars` (stacked, grouped, negative), `Chart.Line`, `Chart.Tooltip` (tap a category), `Chart.YAxis` and `Chart.XAxis`, and `Chart.Table`, the numbers behind a toggle for a screen reader. Bars, grid and labels are Views placed in percent; the line is `react-native-svg` (an optional peer, needed only for this subpath and badges).
