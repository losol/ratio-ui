`@eventuras/ratio-ui/fx` plays `fireworks` and `lightning`. Lightning draws its bolt with a glow, lights the page through the model's flash cap (at most three flashes a second, under white-out, shared by every effect on the page), and with `shake` shakes the element it strikes on top of its own transform. `sound: true` adds thunder, a low rumble of filtered noise, only after the reader has interacted with the page and only under full motion.
