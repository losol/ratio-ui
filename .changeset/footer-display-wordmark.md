`Footer.Brand` takes `display`: the wordmark very large and light, as the footer's closing line instead of a label. An `<em>` inside `name` is set in the accent italic, e.g. `name={<>Kunnskaps<em>kollektivet</em></>}`. It scales with the viewport and wraps on a narrow one. On a `primary`, `secondary` or `accent` surface the `<em>` keeps the band's ink.
