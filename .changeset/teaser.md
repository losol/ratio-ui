New `Teaser`: one story as a link — picture, eyebrow, title and lead, in the magazine look. Three variants: `md`, a card for a grid; `lg`, the lead story with a 16:9 picture and a display-size title; and `row`, a compact line with a small picture on the right, for a list of the next courses or the latest in a topic. The picture is any image element, fitted to the frame; `LinkComponent` takes a router link; `titleAs` sets the heading level (default `h3`). Plain markup and CSS from the semantic tokens, server-safe.
