New `Meter` in `@eventuras/ratio-ui/visuals/Progress`: a thin bar for a value against its ceiling (registrations against capacity), with `tone` and `thresholds` that change it as the value climbs, `role="meter"` with `aria-valuenow`/`aria-valuemax`/`aria-valuetext`, and `height`. `meterTone` resolves the tone for a fraction.
