`Badge` takes `tone="inherit"` (beta): the host's text colour on a
translucent tint of it. Compose a count inside a button, tab or link and it
matches its host in every variant and theme, e.g. a checkout button:
`<Button icon={<ShoppingCart />}>Checkout <Badge variant="count" tone="inherit">3</Badge></Button>`.
