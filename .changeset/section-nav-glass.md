`SectionNav` takes `glass` and `size`. `glass` is the page surface at 88% with
a backdrop blur, the treatment `Navbar` already has — a detail page stacks the
two rows, and with one opaque and one frosted they read as different
materials. `size="sm"` trims the row from 3rem to 2.5rem; a pinned row is
viewport the reader never gets back, and the labels are the same size either
way, only the air around them changes. Both default to today's behaviour.
