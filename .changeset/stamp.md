`Stamp`: a word in capitals in a frame, at a slant, as a rubber stamp lands on a paper that has been through the office. Ink only, in the primary or a status colour (`tone`), two sizes, `tilt` in degrees, and `decorative` for a stamp whose word the page already says. The frame, face, corner and slant are the `--stamp-*` tokens; Tabula stamps thicker, square, in its condensed face.
