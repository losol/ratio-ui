New `@eventuras/ratio-ui-native/badges` (beta): `AchievementBadge` and `AchievementBadgeArt` draw an Open Badges achievement as a medallion in the app, through `react-native-svg` (an optional peer dependency, needed only for this subpath), from the same `BadgeDisplay` and the same outline geometry as the web — four shapes, three metals, a glyph or a symbol, a caption, and the earned, locked and in-progress states, in `tile`, `row` and `art` layouts. The ink is the theme's primary and the paper its card. The data and baking from `@eventuras/ratio-ui-core/badges` are re-exported.
