`Timeline` (beta) gains what an activity feed needs: `Timeline.Group` with a `label` for day or phase headers, `gutter` on the root to put every item's timestamp in a column left of the rail, and `layout="activity"` on an item, where the title is the sentence (actor in bold, target a link) and children the detail line. `TimelineGroupProps`, `TimelineItemLayout` and `TimelineMarker` are exported.
