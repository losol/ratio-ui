New `@eventuras/ratio-ui-core/charts` (beta): the arithmetic of a chart, shared by every renderer. `niceTicks` and `niceStep` put an axis on nice numbers and widen the domain to whole steps; `linearScale` and `bandScale` map values and categories to a fraction 0–1 of the plot; `stackSeries` stacks positives up and negatives down from zero; `valueExtent` measures what stacks and lines reach; `numberFormatter` writes ticks with the step's decimals in the reader's locale.
